# Project Rules & Design Guidelines

## Visual Identity & Aesthetics
- **No purple gradients:** Never use purple, violet, or magenta gradients anywhere in the codebase.
- **Palette:** Clinical medical theme: deep navy, slate, clinical teal (`#0d9488`), and precision cyan/cobalt blue.
- **No vibe-coded tropes:** No floating neon orbs, glowing purple card borders, or cliché AI styling.
- **No cursor animations:** Standard system cursor only.
- **No over-the-top scroll animations:** Standard clean scrolling without parallax distortion or scroll jacking.

## Iconography & Assets
- **No emoji icons:** Replace all emojis in UI buttons, headings, and badges with clean SVG icons from `lucide-react`.
- **No AI slop photos:** Use clean medical UI components, SVG diagrams, and authentic radiological UI.
- **Dedicated Favicon:** Always use a crisp medical brain SVG favicon, never the default Vite or framework logo.

## Copywriting & Transparency
- **No em dashes (`—`):** Do not use em dashes in visible UI copy, titles, or marketing text. Use hyphens (`-`), colons, or clean phrasing.
- **No fake hero text or AI slop copy:** Factual, clinical, professional language only.
- **No fake reviews, fake metrics, or fake counters:** Zero fabricated customer counters or fake doctor quotes.
- **No "Made with AI" tags:** Never include "Made with AI", "Built by AI", or AI framework watermarks in UI or footers.

## Mandatory Pages Before Launch
- Custom domain connection guide and configuration.
- Valid favicon.
- Removal of AI-generated watermarks.
- Privacy Policy page at `/privacy`.
- Terms and Conditions page at `/terms`.
